# Privacy

BODYMADE runs without an account, backend, analytics, or telemetry. Profile settings, workout sets, food logs, recovery notes, and weigh-ins are stored in this browser's IndexedDB. A browser reset, private-browsing session, or clearing site storage can remove them.

The app uses a system font stack and makes no third-party requests. The service worker caches the application shell and same-origin app assets for offline use.

Use **Export data** in Settings to download a versioned JSON backup. Import validates the schema, previews record counts, and lets you merge or replace local records. **Delete local data** removes BODYMADE's local database. This action cannot be undone unless you exported a backup.

Avoid saving information you do not want stored in this browser profile. BODYMADE does not provide medical diagnosis or emergency services.
